# Med Spa Site Audit — Loom Script Template

A 2-minute personalized Loom is the conversion asset for this campaign. It replaces the "I built you a free demo site" wedge from the plumber play. Goal: prospect watches it, replies, books a call.

## Pre-record checklist (≤5 min per prospect)

Open their site in Chrome incognito and check these in order. Pick the **3 most expensive issues** for the Loom — never read all 7.

1. **Mobile-first load** (Lighthouse mobile score) — anything under 60 is a goldmine
2. **Booking flow** — click "Book Now" / "Schedule" — does it load fast? Open in modal? Mobile-friendly?
3. **Above-the-fold hero on iPhone** — is the first impression photo-quality, branded, fast?
4. **Service pages** — do they have prices? Schema markup? (View Source → look for `LocalBusiness` or `MedicalBusiness` JSON-LD)
5. **Before/after gallery** — present? Fast-loading? Lazy-loaded?
6. **Google Business Profile vs site** — is the site's NAP/hours consistent with GBP?
7. **Instagram embed / link integration** — does the site reflect the IG aesthetic?

## Loom recording structure (target: 2 minutes flat)

### 0:00–0:15 — Open with credibility, not pitch
> "Hey [first name], it's Vince. I run a small consultancy that works mostly with med spas in the Phoenix/Scottsdale area. I was looking at your site at [domain] this morning and there are 3 things I want to flag because they're costing you bookings — let me show you."

**Avoid:** "I'd love to set up a call to talk about how I can help you grow your business."

### 0:15–0:55 — Issue #1 (the most expensive one)
Share screen showing the issue live. Quantify it.
> "First thing — when I open your site on iPhone, the booking page takes [X] seconds to load. Industry data says every 1-second mobile delay drops conversions about 7%. If you're getting [N] booking page hits a month, that's roughly [X] lost appointments."

### 0:55–1:25 — Issue #2 (mid-weight, technical)
> "Second — your service pages don't have schema markup, which means Google Maps doesn't show your prices when someone is comparison-shopping. About 30% of high-intent local search clicks now come through that surface. Easy fix."

### 1:25–1:50 — Issue #3 (visible, easy to fix)
> "Third — your hero photo is [X]MB on mobile. The Wix template is shipping the desktop image to phones. Your before/afters are gorgeous so let's show that off without the page weight."

### 1:50–2:00 — Soft close
> "If you want, I can put together a one-page write-up of these three plus a couple more I noticed, no charge. Just reply 'send it' and I'll have it over to you tomorrow. Either way — really love what you're doing with the brand."

**Avoid:** "Book a 30-minute discovery call here." Save the Calendly for the follow-up after they engage with the audit.

## After recording

- Loom title: `[Practice name] — quick site audit (2 min)`
- Loom thumbnail: capture a frame showing their actual site, not your face
- Send via Instantly as a follow-up to the cold email — embed the Loom thumbnail link directly
- Track Loom views via Loom's built-in analytics

## Common pitfalls

- **Don't sound rehearsed.** Pause, stumble a bit. The peer/auditor frame breaks if you sound polished.
- **Don't mention price** in the Loom. The audit is the value; pricing comes after they reply.
- **Don't oversell.** 3 issues is enough. If you list 8, you sound desperate.
- **Don't show your demo site.** This isn't the "built-it-already" play. Resist the urge.
- **Don't compliment the site.** Fake compliments destroy the auditor frame. Compliment the BRAND or IG, then pivot to "but the site isn't matching that yet."
